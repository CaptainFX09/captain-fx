Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. The arrangement

Captain FX ("the desk", "we", "us") accepts client capital for the purpose of forex trading under a fixed profit-sharing arrangement. By creating an account you agree to deposit capital on the terms described here and on the rest of this site.

2. Minimum deposit

The minimum deposit to open an account is $100, sent in USDT on the TRC20 network only. Deposits are credited to your account balance only after the transaction is confirmed on-chain.

3. Profit and loss sharing

Trading results are calculated on your allocated capital and split 60% to the client and 40% to the desk. This split applies identically to profits and to losses — a negative result reduces your account balance in the same proportion.

4. Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests are accepted through a monthly window opening on the 1st of each month, against your available account balance at that time. Requests are manually reviewed before approval and are paid to the TRC20 wallet address you provide.

5. No guaranteed returns

Nothing in these terms, on this site, or in any communication from Captain FX constitutes a guarantee of profit or of capital preservation. Forex trading carries a material risk of loss, up to and including the loss of your entire deposited capital.

6. Account accuracy

You are responsible for providing accurate deposit transaction hashes and withdrawal wallet addresses. Captain FX is not responsible for funds sent to an incorrect address, on an incorrect network, or with incorrect transaction details.

7. Termination

Either party may close the arrangement. On closure, your remaining available balance is paid out through the standard withdrawal process, subject to the monthly withdrawal window.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of your account after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and the TRC20 wallet address you provide for withdrawals. We also record your deposit and withdrawal requests and the resulting account balance history.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your client account
  • To verify deposits and process withdrawal requests
  • To send account and transaction notifications
  • To respond to support requests

3. What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with anyone outside of what is required to operate your account and process your transactions.

4. Data storage

Account data is stored with our database provider using industry-standard access controls. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never visible to us in plain text.

5. Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or request that your account be closed and your data removed, subject to any records we are required to retain for financial record-keeping.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related request, please reach out through the contact details at the bottom of this page.

Risk Disclosure

Last updated: September 2026

Trading risk

Forex trading is highly speculative and involves a significant risk of loss. Past performance of any trading strategy or account is not indicative of future results. You should only deposit capital you can afford to lose.

No guarantee of profit

The 60/40 profit split describes how a trading result is divided — it is not a promise of what that result will be. Some months may produce a loss, which is shared in the same proportion as a profit.

Liquidity and withdrawal timing

Withdrawals are processed through a monthly window and are subject to manual review. This means your capital is not available on demand in the same way as a bank account.

Not a bank or regulated fund

Captain FX is a managed trading arrangement between you and the desk. It is not a bank, is not FDIC or similarly insured, and is not a licensed or regulated investment fund. Deposits are not protected by any deposit insurance scheme.

Independent advice

Nothing on this site is financial or investment advice. You are encouraged to seek independent financial advice before depositing capital, and to only invest an amount you are fully prepared to lose.

This page is a plain-language summary for clients and is not a substitute for professional legal advice. If you are operating Captain FX as a business, have this page (and the Terms & Privacy pages above) reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction before relying on them.
