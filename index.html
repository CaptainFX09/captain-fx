Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. The arrangement

PipZoNe ("the desk", "we", "us") accepts client capital for the purpose of forex trading under a fixed profit-sharing arrangement. By creating an account you agree to deposit capital on the terms described here and on the rest of this site.

2. Minimum deposit

The minimum deposit to open an account is $100, sent in USDT on the TRC20 network only. Deposits are credited to your account balance only after the transaction is confirmed on-chain and manually approved.

3. Profit and loss sharing

Trading results are calculated on your allocated capital and split 60% to the client and 40% to the desk. Daily trading results may be recorded and updated to client accounts at approximately 10:00 PM. This split applies to the trading result according to the agreed arrangement. A negative trading result can reduce your account balance.

4. Withdrawals

The minimum withdrawal amount is $10. Available profit may be requested on demand when reflected as withdrawable in your account. The original deposited principal becomes eligible for withdrawal 40 days after the individual deposit is approved. The 40-day period is calculated separately for each approved deposit.

Withdrawal requests are manually reviewed before approval. Once approved, funds are typically received within 5 minutes to 8 hours, paid to the TRC20 wallet address you provide.

5. No guaranteed returns

Nothing in these terms, on this site, or in any communication from PipZoNe constitutes a guarantee of profit or of capital preservation. Forex trading carries a material risk of loss, up to and including the loss of your entire deposited capital.

6. Account accuracy

You are responsible for providing accurate deposit transaction hashes and withdrawal wallet addresses. PipZoNe is not responsible for funds sent to an incorrect address, on an incorrect network, or with incorrect transaction details.

7. Termination

Either party may close the arrangement. On closure, any remaining withdrawable balance is processed according to the applicable withdrawal eligibility rules, including the 40-day principal period where applicable.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of your account after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Privacy Policy

When you create an account we collect your full name, email address, phone number, and the TRC20 wallet address you provide for withdrawals. We also record your deposit and withdrawal requests and the resulting account balance history.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your client account

Risk Disclosure

Last updated: September 2026

Trading risk

Forex trading is highly speculative and involves a significant risk of loss. Past performance of any trading strategy or account is not indicative of future results. You should only deposit capital you can afford to lose.

No guarantee of profit

The 60/40 profit split describes how a trading result is divided — it is not a promise of what that result will be. Some trading periods may produce a loss.

Profit withdrawal availability

Available profit can be requested on demand, subject to account balance availability and manual review. There is no monthly 1st-of-the-month withdrawal window.

Principal withdrawal timing

Deposited principal becomes eligible 40 days after the corresponding deposit is approved. Each deposit has its own approval date and eligibility period.

Not a bank or regulated fund

PipZoNe is a managed trading arrangement between you and the desk. It is not a bank, is not FDIC or similarly insured, and is not a licensed or regulated investment fund. Deposits are not protected by any deposit insurance scheme.

Independent advice

Nothing on this site is financial or investment advice. You are encouraged to seek independent financial advice before depositing capital, and to only invest an amount you are fully prepared to lose.
