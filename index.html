How It Works

Simple. Transparent. Structured.

PipZoNe is designed to keep the client experience simple while maintaining clear trading and withdrawal rules.

01 — Register

Create your client account and complete the registration information.

02 — Deposit

Deposit at least $100 USDT through the supported TRC20 network and submit your transaction proof.

03 — Managed Trading

Your approved capital is reflected in your dashboard and managed according to the trading arrangement.

04 — Track

Monitor your balance, deposited capital, profit and withdrawal history from your dashboard.

05 — Withdraw

Available profit can be requested on demand. Original deposited capital unlocks after 40 days.

Important Information

Understand the rules.

Profit Withdrawals

Available client profit can be requested on demand, subject to account balance and approval.

Principal Lock

Original deposited capital becomes withdrawable 40 days after the deposit is credited to the account.

Minimum Withdrawal

The minimum withdrawal request is $20 USDT.

Withdrawal Fee

A fixed $2 fee is deducted from every approved withdrawal. Example: request $50 → fee $2 → receive $48.
