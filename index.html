Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

1. The arrangement

PipZoNe ("the desk", "we", "us") accepts client capital for the purpose of forex trading under a fixed profit-sharing arrangement. By creating an account you agree to deposit capital on the terms described here and on the rest of this site.

2. Minimum deposit

The minimum deposit to open an account is $100, sent in USDT on the TRC20 network only. Deposits are credited to your account balance only after the transaction is confirmed on-chain.

3. Profit and loss sharing

Trading results are calculated on your allocated capital and split 60% to the client and 40% to the desk. Daily trading results are recorded and updated to client accounts every day at 10:00 PM. This split applies identically to profits and to losses — a negative result reduces your account balance in the same proportion.

4. Withdrawals

The minimum withdrawal amount is $20. A fixed withdrawal fee of $2 is deducted from every withdrawal request; the remaining amount is paid out to you. Your accumulated profit share may be withdrawn on demand, at any time. Your original deposited capital (principal) may only be withdrawn once 40 days have passed since it was credited to your account. Requests are manually reviewed before approval. Once approved, funds are typically received within 5 minutes to 8 hours, paid to the TRC20 wallet address you provide.

5. No guaranteed returns

Nothing in these terms, on this site, or in any communication from PipZoNe constitutes a guarantee of profit or of capital preservation. Forex trading carries a material risk of loss, up to and including the loss of your entire deposited capital.

6. Account accuracy

You are responsible for providing accurate deposit transaction hashes and withdrawal wallet addresses. PipZoNe is not responsible for funds sent to an incorrect address, on an incorrect network, or with incorrect transaction details.

7. Termination

Either party may close the arrangement. On closure, your remaining available balance is paid out through the standard withdrawal process, subject to the principal lock-in period described above.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of your account after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Risk Disclosure

Last updated: September 2026

Trading risk

Forex trading is highly speculative and involves a significant risk of loss. Past performance of any trading strategy or account is not indicative of future results. You should only deposit capital you can afford to lose.

No guarantee of profit

The 60/40 profit split describes how a trading result is divided — it is not a promise of what that result will be. Some days may produce a loss, which is shared in the same proportion as a profit.

Liquidity and withdrawal timing

Your profit share can be requested for withdrawal at any time and is subject to manual review. Your original deposited capital is locked for 40 days from the date it is credited. Once a withdrawal is approved, funds are typically received within 5 minutes to 8 hours. This means your principal is not available on demand in the same way as a bank account.

Not a bank or regulated fund

PipZoNe is a managed trading arrangement between you and the desk. It is not a bank, is not FDIC or similarly insured, and is not a licensed or regulated investment fund. Deposits are not protected by any deposit insurance scheme.

Independent advice

Nothing on this site is financial or investment advice. You are encouraged to seek independent financial advice before depositing capital, and to only invest an amount you are fully prepared to lose.

This page is a plain-language summary for clients and is not a substitute for professional legal advice. If you are operating PipZoNe as a business, have this page (and the Terms & Privacy pages above) reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction before relying on them.
