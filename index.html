How it works

From deposit to settlement, in four steps.

Every account follows the same process. Nothing is settled until the month closes and the actual trading result is verified.

01
Create your account
Register with your details and verify your email.
02
Deposit capital
Send a minimum of $100 in USDT (TRC20) to your account's deposit address. Funds are credited after confirmation.
03
Capital is traded
Your funds are deployed on the forex markets under the agreed trading structure.
04
Monthly settlement
At month end, the actual result is calculated and split 60% to you and 40% to the desk.

Before you deposit

Understand the arrangement.

Returns are not guaranteed

Forex trading can produce losses as well as profits.

Withdrawals follow a monthly window

Withdrawal requests open on the 1st of each month against available balance.

Deposits are confirmed on-chain

A deposit is only credited after its blockchain transaction is verified.

No guaranteed-income claims

Nothing on this site is a promise of a fixed return.

Common questions

FAQ

What's the minimum deposit?+

$100 in USDT (TRC20) to open an account.

How is the profit split calculated?+

At month close, the actual trading result is calculated. You receive 60% and the desk receives 40%.

When can I withdraw?+

Withdrawal requests open on the 1st of every month.

Is my capital guaranteed?+

No. Forex trading can result in losses.
